COMPREHENSIVE PSYCHIATRIC AND NEUROPSYCHOLOGICAL CONSULTATION REPORT
Date of Consultation: March 15, 2026
Patient: PT-9042 | Demographics: 12-year-old male, 6th grade

History of Presenting Concern:
Master PT-9042 presented for an outpatient neuropsychiatric consultation accompanied by his parents following ongoing recommendations from his school guidance counselor. The family describes increasing difficulty over the past academic year with sustaining attention during classroom instruction, marked organizational forgetfulness, and acute frustration when faced with multi-step written assignments. His classroom teachers report that while he demonstrates strong intellectual curiosity and verbal reasoning during open class discussions, his independent desk work is severely compromised by distractibility, fidgeting with desk supplies, and frequent requests to leave his seat.

Diagnostic Examination & Clinical Impressions:
During the clinical interview, the patient was polite, engaging, and cooperative, though exhibited notable motor restlessness and frequent postural shifts throughout the 90-minute session. Psychometric screening and standardized behavioral rating scales completed across home and school environments show clinically elevated T-scores across inattentive and hyperactive-impulsive domains. These objective measures, combined with longitudinal developmental history, substantiate a confirmed clinical diagnosis of Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD), Combined Presentation, of moderate severity. Additionally, the patient exhibited mild anticipatory anxiety regarding test performance, with reported somatic complaints of muscle tension and abdominal butterflies before examinations, consistent with a secondary mild generalized anxiety condition. Depressive symptomatology and panic disorder were thoroughly evaluated and actively ruled out.

Recommendations and Management Plan:
A multimodal management protocol was established in collaboration with the family:
1. Educational Accommodations: It is strongly recommended that the school district implement a formal 504 Plan or Individualized Education Program (IEP) offering 1.5x extended time on timed examinations, chunked assignment deadlines, and preferential seating in close proximity to direct instruction to attenuate environmental distractions.
2. Psychosocial Interventions: Initiate weekly outpatient Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) focusing on compensatory executive functioning routines, time-management scaffolding, and relaxation techniques for performance anxiety.
3. Medical Management: Referral for pediatric psychopharmacological consultation to discuss first-line stimulant medications (e.g., methylphenidate preparations) to optimize classroom focus. Follow-up evaluation is requested in 12 weeks to assess educational accommodations and treatment efficacy.
