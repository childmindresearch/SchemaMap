ADULT DIAGNOSTIC INTAKE AND CLINICAL PROGRESSION SUMMARY
Date: May 18, 2026
Patient: PT-5294 | Demographics: 28-year-old female, Software Quality Engineer

Clinical Evaluation & Narrative Overview:
PT-5294 self-referred to the behavioral health clinic seeking evaluation for pervasive burnout, attentional lapses at work, and cyclical low mood. She reports a lifelong history of struggling with project completion, procrastination, and time blindness, which has become acutely unmanageable following a recent promotion involving independent workload management. She articulated feeling overwhelmed by open-ended deliverables, leading to profound self-criticism, late-night hyperfocus sessions, and subsequent daytime cognitive fatigue.

Diagnostic Findings:

Formal diagnostic evaluation confirms Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder, Predominantly Inattentive Presentation, of moderate severity.
